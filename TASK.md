# Task (60 minutes)

Our billing team tracks insurance claims in a spreadsheet. They need a simple
claims screen in this app, backed by Supabase.

**Your job is not to write the code. Your job is to design and run the AI agent
setup that builds it, and to prove the result is correct.**

1. **Find out what's needed.** The requirements are not written down. Your
   interviewer is the product owner. Ask them.
2. **Design your agent setup.** Which agents and models, what role each one has,
   how work is handed between them, and how they check each other. Put the
   setup in this repo: instructions, agent definitions, scripts, whatever you
   choose.
3. **Run it.** Step in only when you have to, and tell us why when you do.
4. **Prove it works.** Show us evidence that the result is correct.
5. **Improve it.** Tell us what you'd change in the setup so the next run goes
   better.

Explain your choices out loud as you go.
